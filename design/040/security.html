A server is someone's house

No company sits in the middle. A person runs each Housecall server: the host.

You're a guest. The host decides who comes in, and can see what's said in the shared rooms.

Your DMs are sealed letters

A one-to-one DM is locked on your device and opened only on your friend's. The host carries the envelope and can't open it.

They can still see who you write to and when. Group chats and calls aren't sealed yet.

You hold the only spare key

Your recovery key gets your DMs back if you lose your devices.

Nobody can reset it for you, not the host and not us. Only join servers run by people you trust.

Joining another server

Every server has its own host and its own rules. Your keys are the same on all of them, so one recovery key covers every server you join. Two hosts who compare notes can tell it is the same person.

We attack-tested this encryption ourselves. No outside firm has audited it yet.
