Your recovery key

These 24 words get your encrypted DMs back if you lose your devices. Write them down and keep them somewhere safe. Nobody can reset them for you, not the host and not us.

    Anyone with these words can read your encrypted DMs. Keep them out of chats and photos.

      Replace your recovery key?

      You'll get new recovery words. Your security key stays the same, so if someone else may have seen your old words, use Reset security key instead. Replacing does not stop old words that have already leaked.

      Reset your security key?

      • Your encrypted messages from before the reset can't be read on any device after this, unless one of your devices still has the old key.
      • Everyone you have an encrypted DM with will see that your security key changed and will need to accept it before they can send to you.
      • Use this if you lost your devices and your recovery key, or if a device with your key was stolen.
